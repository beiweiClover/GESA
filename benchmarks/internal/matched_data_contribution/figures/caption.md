**Figure S5. Effect of generative-molecule supervision under controlled training-data replacement.** (a) Training-data composition. Both arms shared the same 6,000 historical GASA-Retro* molecules. The remaining 6,000 molecules were either additional historical GASA-Retro* molecules or property-matched molecules generated by TarPass/MolGenBench models. Each arm contained 6,000 ES and 6,000 HS molecules. Seven molecular properties were matched within classes, and distinct non-chiral scaffold-key counts were matched within each replicate while allowing scaffold identities to differ. (b) The same GESA architecture was trained separately on each arm using identical pretrained encoder weights, a fixed training seed, and the same training and validation-selection settings, then evaluated on the same TS1–TS4 molecules. Bars and error bars show the mean and sample standard deviation over three independently sampled training-data pairs; AUROC is plotted from 0.5, the random-ranking reference, and numerical labels show means. HS was the positive class. Test-cohort uncertainty was assessed separately with 2,000 paired class-stratified bootstrap replicates per fitted pair and is provided in the source data.
